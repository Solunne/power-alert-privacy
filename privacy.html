Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Power Alert (package com.solunne.poweralert), also known in French as Alerte secteur.

1. Who I am

I am Sébastien Moreau, the developer of this app. Privacy contact: sebastien.moreau@gmail.com

2. What the app does

Power Alert monitors whether an Android device is plugged into mains power. When a power loss (or restore) is confirmed, it can send alert SMS to phone numbers you configure. Monitoring runs as a foreground service with an ongoing notification.

3. SMS — send only

  • The app requests only the SEND_SMS permission.
  • It sends alert (or test) SMS only to numbers you entered.
  • It does not read your inbox, conversations, or incoming SMS.
  • It does not share SMS content or your numbers with any application server.
  • It does not use SMS to geolocate or track you.
  • Your mobile carrier treats outbound SMS like any other SMS (charges may apply).

4. Data stored on your device

The following stay on your phone only (app local storage):

  • Recipients, SMS templates, location label, settings
  • Monitoring state, SMS counters
  • Outbound SMS history (Premium)
  • Terms acceptance version, local Premium unlock flag

This information is not uploaded to any remote application server. Uninstalling the app removes it (subject to Android backup settings if any).

5. Internet, Google Play and Premium

  • Day-to-day monitoring and SMS are designed to work without any server.
  • Internet is used for Premium purchase / restore via Google Play Billing (product premium_unlock).
  • Google may process payment and account data under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Install statistics available to the developer come from Google Play Console only — not from in-app analytics SDKs.
  • No ads, no sale of personal data.

6. Permissions (summary)

  • SMS — send alerts to your recipients
  • Notifications — ongoing monitoring status
  • Foreground service (special use) — keep power monitoring active
  • Boot completed — restart monitoring after reboot if enabled
  • Battery optimization exemption (request) — reduce missed outages
  • Internet / Play Billing — Premium purchase and restore

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

8. Changes

I may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact
